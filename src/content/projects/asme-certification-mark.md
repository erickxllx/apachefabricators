The ASME Certification Mark is the mark applied to work built under the Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code. It is granted only to shops operating an ASME-accepted quality control system, and it is the mark the designators such as the U Stamp are applied with.

Acceptance is not a one-time event. The quality control manual describes how design, materials, welding, examination, testing and documentation are controlled, and a joint review with ASME and an Authorized Inspection Agency confirms the shop actually works that way before the certificate is renewed.

In practice it means the procedures behind a code job are fixed and auditable: qualified procedures, qualified welders, traceable material and an Authorized Inspector with the authority to reject work.
