ISO 45001 is the international standard for occupational health and safety management systems. It sets out how an organization identifies hazards, controls risk and improves worker safety in a way an auditor can verify.

Certification means our safety program is not a set of intentions. Hazard identification, incident reporting, training records, corrective action and management review all run to a documented system, and that system is audited by an accredited body rather than assessed by us.

For a buyer, the practical effect is that the safety record behind a bid is auditable. Our EHS program, including the SAFE Way pre-task review, the 20-20-20 habit and the Stop Work Obligation, operates inside this framework.
