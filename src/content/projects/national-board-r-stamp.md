The R Stamp authorizes repair and alteration of pressure-retaining items under the National Board Inspection Code. Where the U Stamp covers building a vessel, the R Stamp covers working on one that is already in service.

Repairs and alterations are carried out to a written procedure, verified by an Authorized Inspector and recorded on an R-1 form registered with the National Board. The item goes back into service with documentation that follows it.

For a plant, this is what makes a turnaround workable: the same shop that fabricates new equipment can legally repair and alter existing equipment, so a damaged vessel does not have to be replaced or shipped elsewhere to be brought back into code compliance.
