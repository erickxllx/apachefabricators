The U Stamp authorizes the new construction of pressure vessels under Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code. A vessel may only carry the stamp when it is designed, fabricated, examined and tested to that code and certified by the manufacturer.

Holding the authorization requires an ASME-accepted quality control system and a joint review with an Authorized Inspection Agency. An Authorized Inspector verifies the work as it proceeds, not after the fact, and signs the Manufacturer's Data Report that accompanies the vessel.

This is the authorization that lets us build code vessels rather than sub-contract them. Design calculations, material traceability, welding procedures and qualified welders all sit inside the same system, in the same shop.
