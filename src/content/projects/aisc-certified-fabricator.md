AISC certification is the structural steel industry's audit of whether a fabricator can consistently produce work to the standard a building requires. It is awarded after an independent audit of the shop, not on the strength of an application.

The audit covers the management system, the fabrication procedures, welding and bolting, material control, inspection and the handling of non-conforming work. Certification is maintained through periodic re-audit, so it reflects how the shop operates now rather than how it operated once.

Many building specifications and public projects require an AISC Certified Fabricator by name. Where they do, this certificate is the document that clears the prequalification.
