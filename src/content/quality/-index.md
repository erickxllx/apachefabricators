  # The three named programmes, lifted out of the prose so a buyer can scan them.
  programs:
    - title: The SAFE Way
      description: Before any task begins, the crew works through a fixed set of questions to surface hazards and controls. What am I about to do? How can it hurt me? What am I going to do about it?

    - title: 20-20-20
      description: Every 20 minutes, take 20 seconds to check 20 feet around you and identify any potential hazard. It is a habit, not a poster, and it is what keeps situational awareness alive through a full shift.

    - title: Stop Work Obligation
      description: Employees do not just have Stop Work Authority at Apache Fabricators, they have a Stop Work Obligation. Every person on site is expected to halt a job that is not right, with no consequence for calling it.

  statement: Everyone goes home the same way they came to work.

  subtitleB: Education and Training
  contentB: All field personnel receive safety training specific to their work environment. Additional training is specified for each job description, which may include site specific orientations and Occupational Safety and Health Administration rules. Field Supervisors carry additional requirements including first aid and CPR, lifting and rigging, forklift and powered platform, PPE assessment, and hazard communication. Supervisors conduct daily job briefings that outline the activities for the day, complete JSAs for all anticipated hazards, and cover equipment inspections and the methods used to work safely.
